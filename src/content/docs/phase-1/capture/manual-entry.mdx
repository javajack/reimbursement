Every field force produces bills no OCR can read: faded thermal auto-rickshaw slips, handwritten dhaba bills, a parking chit torn in half. The answer is a manual path engineered to the same standard as the scanner: it opens warm with whatever extraction salvaged, finishes a 3-line claim in under 90 seconds on the Galaxy A24-class P75 baseline device, and feeds the identical downstream workflow. Expensify keeps a permanent "Fill out details myself" escape hatch on every receipt precisely so OCR stays a draft, never a gate; we adopt that stance wholesale. This page picks up where [OCR itemisation](/phase-1/capture/ocr-itemisation/) ends, at the `extraction_failed` queue.

## Three doors into one composer

There is exactly one line composer. Three routes open it; none creates a special "manual claim" object. A Claim is a Claim, whatever typed its lines.

| Entry point | Where it lives | What pre-fills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scan-failed queue, one tap | A Receipt lands in `extraction_failed` with a plain-language reason (blurry, several bills in one photo, unreadable print); the row offers Retry and "Enter manually". | Everything salvage recovered: merchant, date, total where legible. The image stays attached. |
| Skip the scan | Secondary action on the capture screen: "No bill? Enter it manually". For per-diem days, mileage, and expenses that never had paper. | Nothing except smart defaults (below). |
| Add a line in review | Inside `review_items` on a scanned bill, "Add line" appends to the extracted list for items the OCR missed. | Claim header context: merchant, date, GSTIN if extracted. |

The recoverable-queue shape is borrowed deliberately from Zoho Expense's "Scan Failed Receipts" bucket, the category's best-documented failure UX (vendor docs, high confidence).

## Warm start: extraction is salvage, not all-or-nothing

A failed extraction usually recovers something. Whatever survived (often total and date) pre-fills the composer in neutral machine styling and turns confirmed only on human touch, the same Rossum-derived semantic as the review screen. Two invariants hold on this path:

- The Receipt and its image never detach. Approvers and finance see the original photo beside hand-typed lines, and the audit trail retains it for the full retention window.
- Failed OCR never consumes a credit; the meter charges once, at submission, however many scans died on the way.

## The composer: one line at a time

Lines edit in a focused bottom row-sheet (the Zoho Itemize pattern), never a dense grid. Each field is tuned for thumbs on a Galaxy A24-class device, the P75 baseline (engineering guidance, high confidence).

| Field | Control | Default | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amount | Numeric keypad first (`inputmode="decimal"`), fixed ₹ prefix | Empty, keypad open on focus | Paise accepted; stored as integer `amount_paise`; the claim total updates live. |
| Category | Icon grid with labels, ordered by tenant frequency then user frequency | Most-used categories in the first row | One tap; "More" expands the admin-enabled set. |
| Date | Date chip, calendar on tap | Today | Backdating allowed within the Policy window; older dates fire `warn` or `block` per rule hardness. |
| Merchant | Text with merchant-memory suggestions | Suggested, never forced | Picking a known merchant also sets the learned category and GST defaults. |
| GST details | Collapsed toggle | Off | Opens per-line taxable value, rate, and tax split; see below. |
| Receipt | Chip: attach or pick from the claim's receipts | The failed Receipt, when arriving from the queue | Per-line association; new photos enter through the same [capture ladder](/phase-1/capture/camera/). |

<Steps>

1. Type the amount; the keypad is already open.

2. Tap a category icon. The grid is ordered by what this company actually claims.

3. Confirm or change the date chip. Today is right most of the time.

4. Optionally toggle GST details or attach a bill photo to the line.

5. Hit "Save and add another", or "Done" to return to the claim.

</Steps>

### GST fields stay behind the toggle

Most field expenses are simple receipts, not tax invoices, so GST entry is opt-in per line. When toggled on: taxable value, rate, CGST and SGST in equal halves intrastate or IGST interstate, and an inclusive/exclusive switch reconciling sum-of-lines against the printed total. Recomputed tax follows Section 170 CGST Act normal rounding, per component, per invoice, so totals match the printed bill to the rupee (statutory, high confidence). A typed GSTIN runs the `GSTIN_FORMAT` checksum on entry; a failure flags the field with a re-check prompt and reaches the approver flagged, never blocks the save. If CGST and SGST disagree we flag the line; we never silently "fix" it.

### Per-line receipt association

A LineItem may reference any Receipt on the Claim: one hotel bill can back three lines (room, food, laundry), or every line can carry its own parking chit. Up to 5 receipt images ride inside a single claim credit; beyond that, 1 more credit per additional 5, the anti-batching fair-use guard documented in [credits and metering](/phase-1/credits-and-metering/).

## Smart defaults that compound

| Default | Mechanic | Why it pays |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Merchant memory | Per-tenant map of merchant to category and GST defaults; first entry teaches it, later entries auto-fill | Zoho's documented precedent: entry gets faster every week, zero model training (vendor docs, high confidence) |
| Repeat last line | Copies the previous line's category, merchant, and date; clears the amount | Multi-line days (three autos, two meals) become amount-only typing |
| Per-diem template | Inserts an allowance line computed from the `PER_DIEM_CAP` policy table (city tier by grade), with the computation shown | No bill exists; the policy table is the evidence |
| Mileage template | Distance in, amount out at the policy's per-km rate; the line shows the formula for the approver | Kills the most error-prone manual arithmetic |

Templates read the live policy tables from [policy and rules](/phase-1/onboarding/policy-and-rules/); a policy edit reprices the next line, not a release.

## Validation at entry, not at rejection

The same Rule engine that guards submission fires while typing, with the same six actions: `allow`, `flag`, `warn`, `cap`, `block`, `route`. Surfacing consequences in the composer is the cheapest fix for the category's most hated approval failure, the late whole-claim bounce.

| While typing | Rule | Inline behavior | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amount exceeds the category cap | `PER_DIEM_CAP` or any `cap` rule | Live preview: "Tier-2 lodging is capped at ₹3,500. You will be reimbursed up to the cap." The line shows its `approved_amount_paise` before submit. | <span class="pill pill--info">cap</span> |
| Amount over the receipt threshold, no bill attached | `RECEIPT_REQUIRED_ABOVE` | Inline: "Bills are required above ₹500 for Meals. Attach a photo, or this line reaches your approver flagged." | <span class="pill pill--flag">flag</span> |
| Date and amount collide with an existing line | `DUPLICATE_SUSPECT` | Dismissible warning with explicit verbs: Keep both / Keep one. | <span class="pill pill--flag">flag</span> |
| Hard policy breach (disabled category, date outside the window) | Any `block` rule with hardness `hard` | Submit disabled, the rule's message shown, "Save as draft" always available. | <span class="pill pill--block">block</span> |

`warn` lets the employee proceed past a soft notice, `route` only changes who approves, and `allow` is silent. Threshold amounts above (₹3,500, ₹500) are example policy values, not product constants. Routing gaps never strand a claim: if no approval rule matches, it lands in the admin queue flagged `rule_gap`. Every rule firing writes an AuditEvent with rule id, version, and input snapshot.

## Vernacular and accessible by construction

The composer serves the least technical employees in the company, so it is numbers-first, icons-first, and labelled in the employee's language. The mandate is demographic: 98% of India's 886M internet users consume Indic-language content and 55% are rural (IAMAI-Kantar 2024, high confidence). Hindi plus 2-3 state languages ship at launch, and the web stack deploys label fixes same-day with no store-release train.

| Moment | English | Hindi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Receipt needed | "Bills are required above ₹500." | "₹500 से ऊपर बिल ज़रूरी है।" |
| Offline save | "Saved on phone. Uploads when you are back online." | "फ़ोन पर सेव हो गया। नेटवर्क आते ही अपलोड होगा।" |

Accessibility is the same discipline: every control carries a screen-reader label, the category grid announces icon names, the keypad announces the running total, and the whole flow works as a linear field list without ever touching an image (AFB AccessWorld patterns, medium confidence).

## Parity: a manual claim is a claim

:::note[Parity is the contract]
Manual submissions consume a credit exactly like scanned ones: the meter is the submission and its workflow, not the OCR. At zero balance a manual claim waits in `awaiting_credits` with everything else; resubmission after `sent_back` stays free; the Free plan's 20 monthly submissions count manual and scanned alike. Same rules, same approval chain, same audit trail.
:::

This is a deliberate departure from Expensify, which meters scans (25 free SmartScans a month, then $0.20) while leaving manual entries unlimited (third-party pricing trackers, medium confidence). Their meter prices OCR; ours prices workflow. An unlimited manual lane would make the meter trivially gameable and would misprice the real cost drivers, which are approvals, storage, exports, and payout-file generation, not extraction: COGS is roughly ₹1.0-2.5 per submission against pack prices of ₹5.00-9.98 per credit (our derivation). The pricing FAQ states it plainly.

## Anti-abuse: manual does not mean unexamined

:::caution[Finance sees the difference]
Manual entry skips extraction, never scrutiny. `RECEIPT_REQUIRED_ABOVE` applies at full force, so typing a line instead of scanning it cannot dodge an evidence threshold. `DUPLICATE_SUSPECT` runs on date and amount collisions regardless of entry method, and perceptual-hash dedupe still covers any attached images. Every line records `entry_method` (`ocr`, `ocr_edited`, `manual`), and the [finance console](/phase-1/web/finance-console/) shows an "Entered manually" badge and filter to drive sampling. The badge informs review; it is never a penalty.
:::

## Done means

- A 3-line claim entered in under 90 seconds on a Galaxy A24-class device, measured composer-open to submit-tap.
- One tap from `extraction_failed` to a warm composer, salvage pre-filled, image attached.
- Amount focus opens a numeric keypad; the common path needs zero free-text typing.
- GST fields hidden by default; toggling on and off never loses entered values.
- Per-diem and mileage templates compute from live policy tables, never hardcoded numbers.
- Every control screen-reader labelled; the full flow completes under TalkBack.
- Submission consumes exactly 1 credit; prior failed scans consumed none.
- `entry_method` recorded per line and surfaced as a finance badge and filter.

With lines in place, the claim moves to submission, status tracking, and the "when do I get paid" answer in [submit and track](/phase-1/capture/submit-and-track/).
