An employee photographs a bill and gets paid on a visible date, with finance in control the whole way. That is the product. This dossier specifies it end to end: a PWA camera flow engineered to document-scanner grade (no stretched bills), OCR that is honest about its confidence with a manual line-item fallback, a configurable maker-checker approval engine routed by levels and amount thresholds, company onboarding that reaches a first approved claim in one session, payouts by bank-file export (with an optional payout API per customer), and a credit meter that charges for submissions while every user and approver logs in free.

It is built from two multi-agent research passes (21 agents on the India market, regulation, and rails; 10 more on capture engineering, OCR UX, usage pricing, onboarding, approvals, and PWA reality in India), each adversarially fact-checked, then synthesized and reviewed. Figures carry their provenance and confidence throughout.

:::tip[The stance, in one line]
<span class="pill pill--build">Conditional Build</span> &nbsp; Pure software, **employee reimbursement first**, India-first. No cards, no float; funds never touch the platform. Unlimited users and approvers free; **submissions are the meter**, with a soft paywall that queues and never loses a bill. Vendors and their TDS/RCM complexity arrive in Phase 2, deliberately.
:::

## The 90-second version

- **The pain is documented, not assumed.** Companies already paying for expense software still hit silently-wrong OCR amounts, minutes-to-hours scan queues, approval black holes, and a category-wide inability to answer "when do I get paid?". Each pillar of this product maps to a cited, unresolved complaint.
- **Capture is the demo.** No major incumbent ships document-scanner-grade capture in its core mobile flow. The stretched-bill bug is an engineering bug class with a canonical fix, and this dossier specifies it down to the canvas math.
- **Pricing is the story.** Per-user fees plus scan meters are actively resented. Unlimited seats with metered submissions undercuts seat pricing for almost every Indian SMB (a 25-person company at an estimated 4 submissions/employee/month pays roughly ₹700/month here versus ₹2,475/month on seat pricing; our derivation, frequency unbenchmarked), at a modelled 67-90% gross margin on a ₹1.0-2.5 per-submission cost base (our derivation, medium confidence).
- **Regulation is designed out, not managed.** Bank-file export means no RBI Payment Aggregator or wallet licence is needed; DPDP is engineered in from day one.
- **The spec is buildable.** Data model, API, state machines, config and rule schemas, and the capture pipeline are specified for LLM coding agents to implement without asking questions.
