Indian employees and vendors still float work expenses on their own money and wait weeks to be repaid: a field agent moving city to city, a team lead covering lunch, a salesperson at a client event, a developer who bought event tickets on a personal card, a contractor raising a claim. The product idea is to digitise that loop end to end: capture the bill, read and validate its GST detail, run it through a configurable policy engine, route it through a maker-checker approval, then pay it out from a bank account the system owner has configured, and post it to the books.

This dossier is the honest answer to a harder question: is that worth building as an indie hacker or a small team, in a market that already exists? It is built from a multi-agent research pass (14 domain studies and 7 adversarial fact-checks) and it commits to a recommendation.

:::tip[The verdict, in one line]
<span class="pill pill--build">Conditional Build</span> &nbsp; Worth building, but only as a software-only, **Tally-native, WhatsApp-first reimbursement product for sub-200-employee Indian SMBs and field-heavy teams**, with money movement partnered out and revenue from software seats, not card float. Not worth building as a horizontal Zaggle or Happay clone, and not as a card-issuing business.
:::

## The 90-second version

- The market is a small but fast-growing niche: a defensible India expense-software figure is roughly **USD 150 to 250 million**, growing **14 to 18% a year**. "Fast-growing" is true; "large" is overstated. No tier-1 analyst publishes an India-only number.
- Incumbents win on distribution, bank partnerships and **card interchange and float** (around three-quarters of the listed leader's revenue), not on better software. An indie team cannot touch that pool without RBI licensing.
- So compete where software still decides: **India-native compliance and integration**. OCR and cards are table-stakes; GST input-credit capture and a rock-solid **Tally** integration are the moat.
- Stay a technology provider on payments: if money never touches an account you control, you need no RBI Payment Aggregator or wallet licence. This is a product invariant, not a detail.
- Get the over-ask right: a lovable MVP is buildable in 3 to 6 months if you defer cards, SOC 2, and deep ERP, and partner out payouts.
