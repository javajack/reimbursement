GTM for this product is decided by arithmetic, not ambition. India SMB ACVs in our band are too small to fund an outbound salesforce, so the motion is product-led at the bottom and relationship-led at the top: a free tier that onboards whole companies, an employee-nudge loop that recruits the buyer from inside the product, content and the CA/Tally channel for low-CAC pipeline, and the founder closing over email, calls, and community. The pricing model in [pricing](/strategy/pricing/) is not adjacent to this plan; it is the distribution engine.

## The free tier is the PLG engine

Every plan, including `Free`, has unlimited seats. That single decision changes what "trial" means in this category. With Zoho's 3-user free cap, a trial is a finance person poking at a sandbox. With unlimited seats plus 20 submissions per company per month, a trial is the WHOLE company live in production: every employee installs the PWA, every approver gets a real inbox, the finance lead runs a real payout file. Nothing about the org is held back; only volume is metered.

The 20-per-month trickle does two jobs at once:

- **It keeps small firms alive forever.** A 5-person shop filing 2-6 claims per employee per month (our estimate, unbenchmarked; see the design-partner section) may never exhaust it. That is fine: they are references, word-of-mouth, and future growers, at a COGS of roughly ₹1.0-2.5 per submission (our derivation from Document AI and OCR+LLM pricing, medium confidence).
- **It creates the upgrade moment mechanically.** A 25-person company at 4 submissions per employee per month burns 100 credits and hits the soft paywall in week one. Claims land in `awaiting_credits`, visibly queued, never lost; capture keeps working. The product converts itself the moment the company grows into it, with no sales touch.

Free-to-paid conversion for tightly targeted B2B tools runs 5-15% versus 1-5% for broad tools (ChartMogul/First Page Sage benchmarks, directional). Our paywall is tighter than a feature gate: it fires exactly when usage proves value. <span class="pill pill--med">Medium</span> confidence on the rate, <span class="pill pill--high">High</span> on the trigger timing, because the trigger is usage itself.

## The employee-nudge loop: distribution inside the product

The soft paywall is also the sales rep. When credits hit zero, every queued bill shows the employee a calm banner ("Your bills are safe; processing resumes when credits are added") and a one-tap "remind your admin" nudge. The admin sees aggregated nudge counts, queue depth, burn forecasts, and a one-click buy. This is the Notion request-button and Figma seat-request pattern applied to expense money, and stronger than either precedent: the value withheld is the employee's own reimbursement, so upgrade pressure is distributed across the whole company while only the owner can pay.

:::caution[The loop cuts both ways]
Delayed reimbursement money is emotionally hotter than hidden Slack history. If admins ignore nudges, employees blame us, not their admin. The permanent free trickle, the 80% and 100% burn alerts, and the "out in ~N days" forecast exist so the paywall is never a surprise; and queued claims are NOT in the approval flow, so month-end exhaustion must be communicated loudly or it becomes a finance incident. Mechanics in [credits and metering](/phase-1/credits-and-metering/).
:::

The arithmetic against outbound is unchanged from v1 and sharper under v2 pricing: a company on the Growth pack spends about ₹700 per month at 100 submissions (our derivation from the canonical ladder). No SDR motion at ₹2-4L CAC ever pays back against that. The whole funnel must be self-serve or near-free, which is exactly what the free tier and the nudge loop provide.

## Design partners first

Before any spend, 10-15 hand-recruited design partners: field-heavy SMBs in the 20-200 employee band (sales teams, service engineers, logistics) where claim volume is real and the capture pain in [pain points](/strategy/pain-points/) is daily.

What we ask of them:

- A weekly call with the finance lead or owner.
- Real claims through the real pipeline, not staged demos: actual thermal-paper bills, actual approvers, actual payout files.
- Frequency telemetry. The 2-6 submissions per employee per month figure that sizes the free tier and the packs is an unbenchmarked training-knowledge estimate. <span class="pill pill--low">Low</span> Design partners are how it becomes a measured number before pack sizes are locked. If real medians are 1-2 per month, the free tier is too generous for sub-10-person firms and the ladder shifts.

What they get: lifetime pricing locked at design-partner rates, direct input on the configuration model and the Tally export, and a named line to the founder. Cheap for us, durable for them, and it produces the reference customers every later channel needs.

## The sequence

<Steps>

1. **Design partners (now).** Prove the wedge in production, measure submission frequency, harvest the first stretched-vs-clean capture comparisons and close-week stories.

2. **Content on the pain canon, with the capture demo as the hero asset.** The pain points page is the editorial calendar: silent capture failures, the approval black hole, "when do I get paid", lost ITC. The hero asset is a 30-second video, a stretched, unreadable bill from an incumbent's capture flow next to our edge-detected, perspective-corrected scan (the no-distortion rule from [the camera spec](/phase-1/capture/camera/) made visible). Capture quality is the one differentiator a finance buyer can SEE in half a minute. Content/SEO CAC of ₹40-80k compounds from here.

3. **The CA and Tally channel, with the JV export as the door-opener.** Tally claims 28,000+ partners (2022, vendor-claimed, not independently audited), many CA-founded and embedded inside SMB accounting. The pitch to a partner is not "refer our software"; it is "your client's reimbursements arrive as a clean Tally journal voucher instead of manual re-keying". The export that makes the product win is also the relationship that distributes it; a per-deal referral fee or revenue share, co-branded onboarding, and a partner-facing connector guide make it concrete. Connector detail in [integrations](/spec/integrations/). Partnership CAC ₹50k-1.5L (same single source).

4. **Marketplace listings.** Zoho Marketplace (free to publish; vendor-claimed 60M+ users across products) and the Tally ecosystem. Top-of-funnel lead-gen off installed bases whose accounting decision is already made; founder-led email, calls, and community close what these surface.

5. **Light inside sales, only after 2-4 produce volume.** Not before.

</Steps>

## Honest channel risks

- **Every CAC number on this page traces to one consultancy benchmark** plus one aggregator blog. Treat the ranking as robust (content and partners beat outbound at these ACVs under any plausible error) and the absolute numbers as soft.
- **The nudge loop can sour.** If the upgrade moment reads as ransom on employees' money, the free trickle and the alert ladder are the only defence. This is a copy and product problem before it is a channel problem.
- **The CA/Tally channel is slow to start and easy to overestimate.** The 28,000-partner figure is vendor marketing; the active, motivated subset is unknown. Referral economics only work once the product needs no hand-holding, so this channel follows design partners, never precedes them.
- **Marketplaces generate browsers, not buyers.** Without founder follow-up they are a vanity metric.
- **Frequency risk is GTM risk.** If submission frequency is materially below 2-6 per employee per month, free-tier exhaustion (the conversion trigger) arrives later or never for small firms, and the funnel math in [unit economics](/strategy/unit-economics/) loosens. This is the single most important thing design partners must measure.
