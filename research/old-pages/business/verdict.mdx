Build it, but only one specific thing. The answer is <span class="pill pill--build">Conditional Build</span>: a software-only, Tally-native, WhatsApp-first reimbursement product for sub-200-employee Indian SMBs and field-heavy verticals, with money movement partnered out and monetised on software seats. The same effort aimed at a horizontal Zaggle clone, a card-and-float business, or a price war against Zoho scores <span class="pill pill--avoid">Avoid</span>. The market is real and the wedge is defensible; the trap is the temptation to broaden it.

This page scores the decision across seven dimensions, states the verdict, then names the precise conditions that flip it from Build to Caution or Avoid. For the condensed one-pager (not the scorecard) see the [executive summary](/overview/executive-summary/); for what actually ships first see the [MVP scope](/product/mvp-scope/).

## The scorecard

Scores are out of 5 (5 = strongly favourable to an indie team). They reflect our synthesis of the feasibility and market research, not a sourced index. Each comment carries the load-bearing reason.

| Dimension | Score | Comment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market attractiveness | 3 / 5 | Real but oversold. India SMB expense-software TAM is a derived ~USD 150-300M with SAM ~USD 80-150M (our derivation, low confidence). No tier-1 analyst publishes an India-only figure. "Fast-growing" is fair (~14-18% range); "large" is not. |
| Competitive intensity | 2 / 5 | Crowded and funded. Zaggle (listed, ~₹1,303 cr FY25 revenue), Happay (now MakeMyTrip), Volopay, plus Pluxee in the adjacent benefits-card lane (not a direct reimbursement-software rival), and Zoho anchoring price near zero. Horizontal window is narrowing as the category consolidates. |
| Defensibility / moat | 4 / 5 | Tally-native two-way sync (XML-over-HTTP port 9000) plus GST ITC capture and WhatsApp-first field UX is genuinely hard to copy and genuinely under-served. Each moat is individually shallow; stacked, they hold. |
| Regulatory burden | 3 / 5 | Manageable if you stay a technology provider. DPDP and ISO 27001 are real but bounded costs. The burden spikes only if you touch money or cards. The invariant keeps the score from dropping. |
| Unit economics | 3 / 5 | Workable on seats, fragile on price. OCR at ~USD 0.0005-0.003/doc and payouts at ~₹1.75-5/txn are cheap; the risk is drifting into low-margin services at the ₹79-149/user/mo point. |
| Founder fit | 4 / 5 | A 2-person team can build this in ~3-6 months by buying OCR, partnering payouts, and investing the scarce engineering on Tally. Fit collapses if the team tries to also be a fintech. |
| Exit potential | 4 / 5 | The category's exit path is proven: Happay to CRED then MakeMyTrip, Fyle to Sage (2025). Clean accounting/ERP integration keeps the asset acquisition-ready by design. |

<p class="source-note">Scores: our synthesis (low-medium confidence). Underlying figures from the canonical set and the feasibility and market-size research (analyst, vendor, and derived as labelled).</p>

The unweighted average sits around 3.3 / 5. That is not a ringing "yes" and it should not be read as one. The number that matters is the spread: defensibility, founder-fit and exit score high precisely because they reward the narrow play, while competitive intensity and market size punish the broad one. The verdict lives in that spread.

## The verdict

<span class="pill pill--build">Conditional Build</span>, with the condition load-bearing, not decorative.

The reasoning, in one breath: incumbents win on distribution, bank partnerships, and card interchange plus float (roughly 75-80% of Zaggle's revenue is interchange, vendor-disclosed), not on better software. An indie team cannot reach that revenue pool without RBI licensing (non-bank PPI net worth ₹5 cr rising to ₹15 cr) or a bank partner. So you compete only where software still decides the deal: India-native compliance depth, Tally integration, field and mobile UX, and reimbursement speed.

:::tip[The wedge that earns the Build]
Tally-native two-way sync is the single biggest deal-maker and deal-breaker (~70-80% Indian SMB accounting, vendor-claimed, not independently audited). It is painful (desktop, on-prem, XML-over-HTTP) precisely because that pain deters competitors. OCR and cards are table-stakes; Tally and GST ITC are the moat. See [the whitespace](/market/whitespace/).
:::

## The conditions that flip it

Each of these turns the verdict negative. They are the predictable failure modes from the feasibility research, restated as gates.

| Condition | Flips to | Why it breaks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trying to go horizontal | <span class="pill pill--avoid">Avoid</span> | A "better expense app" has no wedge against funded, consolidating incumbents (Happay to MakeMyTrip; Volopay and Kodo with USD 9-33M raised). The horizontal window is closing, not opening. |
| Building cards / PPI / float | <span class="pill pill--avoid">Avoid</span> | The interchange pool needs RBI licensing or a bank partner and a balance sheet. You lose to float economics you structurally cannot match. RBI's Feb 2024 clampdown on card-based B2B via intermediaries makes it worse. |
| Under-pricing into services | <span class="pill pill--caution">Caution</span> | At ₹79-149/user/mo with high support load, a 2-person team drifts into low-margin implementation work. Cap services; monetise seats, not hours. |
| No Tally depth | <span class="pill pill--avoid">Avoid</span> | Remove Tally and you are a thin receipt-capture-and-approve tool that Zoho already anchors near zero. Tally depth is the difference between a product and a feature. |

:::danger[Regulatory invariant, not negotiable]
Funds must never touch an account the SaaS owns or controls. Use file hand-off to the customer's own corporate bank, a regulated partner (RazorpayX or Cashfree) initiating from its own escrow, or connected banking on the customer's current account. Hold or pool funds and you trigger PA (net worth ₹15 cr at application, ₹25 cr by year 3) or PPI licensing. Get a written legal opinion before go-live.
:::

Two further caution flags worth naming. Hitting the certification wall (ISO 27001 ~₹5 L, SOC 2 ~₹6-14 L) on a first mid-market deal with no runway can stall the company; sequence ISO 27001 first and defer SOC 2 until a US-HQ deal funds it. And broadening the surface prematurely (deep ERP, own cards, travel booking) before a paying deal funds it converts a tight roadmap into an unbounded one.

## Who should build this

<span class="pill pill--build">Build</span> if you are:

- A small, finance-literate, India-based team willing to do the unglamorous Tally engineering that foreign tools (Fyle/Sage integrates QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite, but not Tally) refuse to.
- Comfortable monetising on software seats at the Zoho-anchored ladder (~₹79-149/user/mo annual, approvers free, 5-user minimum) and partnering all money movement.
- Patient with a realistic indie SOM of ~USD 0.5-2M ARR over three years (our derivation, low confidence), and treating maker-checker, GST ITC, and a single vertical as first-class features.
- Planning, eventually, to extend toward vendor-payments and AP automation, a larger and GST-mandate-driven market, which is also where the [whitespace](/market/whitespace/) compounds.

## Who should not

<span class="pill pill--avoid">Do not build</span> if you are:

- Hoping to out-fund Zaggle, Happay, or Volopay on a horizontal spend-management play. You will not, and the unit economics on the card layer are closed to you.
- Drawn to corporate cards, PPI, or float as the business model. That is a licensed, capital-intensive fintech, not an indie SaaS.
- Unwilling to invest in Tally-native sync, or planning to win on price alone against Zoho's bundled, near-zero floor. Without compliance depth, there is no moat and no margin.
- Expecting a large, ready-made market. The India-only software market is fast-growing but modest, the pain stats are largely vendor-sourced and dramatized, and employee-voice evidence is under-sampled (primary interviews are recommended before building).

The honest one-liner: this is a good business for the right two people doing exactly one narrow thing, and a bad business for almost everyone trying to do more. Hold the line on the wedge and the verdict holds at <span class="pill pill--build">Build</span>. Start the build sequence at the [MVP scope](/product/mvp-scope/).
