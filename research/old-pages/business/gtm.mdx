For an indie team selling reimbursement software to Indian SMBs, the GTM is decided by arithmetic, not ambition. India SMB ACVs sit around ₹4-8 lakh for funded firms and far lower for the sub-200-employee band this product targets, so any channel with a CAC above roughly ₹1.5 lakh loses money. That single constraint rules out the outbound SDR motion (CAC ₹2-4 lakh) and rules in content, marketplace listings, the CA and Tally-partner referral channel, and founder-led sales. <span class="pill pill--build">Build cheaply</span>

:::tip[The motion in one line]
Win design partners by hand, publish India-tax-and-Tally content that ranks, list on the marketplaces your buyer already lives in, recruit CAs and Tally resellers as referrers, and run founder-led WhatsApp and email sales. Add light inside sales only after the first channels prove repeatable.
:::

## The channels, ranked by CAC

The CAC figures below are from a single India consultancy benchmark (upGrowth) calibrated to the ₹10-30 crore ARR band. A pre-revenue indie team will see different, usually lower-ticket economics, so treat these as directional, not audited.

| Channel | CAC per customer | Fit for indie | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Content / SEO | ₹40-80k | Strong | Compounds over time; ranks on GST ITC, Tally sync, maker-checker queries |
| Tally-partner + CA / accountant referral | ₹50k-1.5L | Strong | Embedded with SMB accounting; trust transfers to the tool |
| Marketplace listings (Zoho Marketplace, Tally partner network) | Near-zero to list | Strong | Free to publish; lead-gen off an installed base |
| Founder-led WhatsApp / email sales | Founder time only | Strong (early) | The default India SMB motion; converts engaged free users |
| SaaSBoomi community | Membership + time | Support, not a channel | 4,000+ founder peer network for GTM learning and warm intros |
| Outbound / SDR | ₹2-4L | Avoid early | Unaffordable at India SMB ACVs |
| ABM | ₹5-12L | Avoid | Enterprise-only economics |

<p class="source-note">Source: upGrowth CAC benchmarks for Indian B2B SaaS, 2026 (analyst, medium confidence); channel fit is our own derivation.</p>

:::caution[Why not outbound SDRs]
At ₹2-4 lakh CAC against sub-₹2 lakh ACVs and an LTV:CAC target of 2.5-3x, an outbound salesforce burns cash on every deal. The math only closes at higher ACVs or with a card-interchange revenue pool, neither of which a software-only indie has. Skip it until a paid motion is already working.
:::

## Content and SEO: the compounding base

Content is the cheapest durable channel (₹40-80k CAC) and the one an indie founder can run alone. The wedge topics double as search demand: GST input-tax-credit capture, blocked ITC under Section 17(5), TDS section mapping (194C/194J), the MSME 45-day payment clock, and Tally two-way sync. These are the exact pains the Finance-led buyer searches for, and they are under-served by neutral, accurate writing because incumbents publish thin marketing.

PLG plus SEO is now the default for sub-USD-10M ARR India SaaS, with reported PLG median CAC of ₹500-1,500 versus ₹2,000-5,000 sales-led (productgrowth.in aggregator, directional only). The lever is a tight free tier that turns content readers into product trials without a sales touch. See [pricing & packaging](/business/pricing/) for the free-tier and paid-trigger design.

## The CA and Tally-partner channel

This is the most India-specific edge. Tally reported 28,000+ partners (2022, vendor-claimed) across 3-star and 5-star certified tiers; many are CA-founded and embedded inside SMB accounting. They already sit between the vendor and the buyer's books, so a referral carries trust that a cold pitch never does. Partnership CAC runs ₹50k-1.5 lakh, competitive with content and far below outbound.

:::note[Why the channel matches the product]
Tally-native two-way sync is the single biggest deal-maker for this product. The same partners who implement Tally are the ones who feel the manual-rekeying pain in their clients, so the channel and the wedge reinforce each other. The integration that makes the product win is also the relationship that distributes it.
:::

A workable referral program: a per-deal referral fee or a revenue share, co-branded onboarding, and a partner-facing setup guide for the Tally connector. The connector reality (desktop, on-prem, XML-over-HTTP on port 9000) is operational pain the partner can absorb on the customer's behalf, which makes them more valuable, not less. Integration depth is covered in [integrations & survival](/business/integrations/).

## Marketplace listings

Two listings cost almost nothing and put the product where the buyer already is.

<Steps>

1. **Zoho Marketplace.** Free to publish, reaching a large Zoho install base (vendor-claimed 60M+ users across products, medium confidence). A Zoho Books and Zoho People integration listing is lead-gen off buyers already committed to cloud accounting.

2. **Tally partner network.** Listing inside the Tally ecosystem reaches the SMBs whose accounting decision is already made, the highest-intent audience for a Tally-native tool.

</Steps>

Marketplaces are top-of-funnel, not a closing motion. They generate qualified inbound that founder-led sales then converts.

## Founder-led sales and community

For the first cohorts, the founder sells. India SMB GTM commonly runs over WhatsApp and email, which is also where managers already negotiate off-system policy exceptions, so the channel meets buyers in their existing habit. Founder-led selling is free in cash and priceless in learning: every objection (weak ERP integration, rigid workflows, data security, price versus spreadsheets) is product feedback.

SaaSBoomi, a 4,000+ India SaaS founder pay-it-forward community, is a learning and warm-intro network rather than a paid channel. Use it for distribution lessons and peer benchmarks, not as a line item in the CAC model.

## The sequence

GTM is staged, not parallel. Each phase funds the next and de-risks it before spend scales.

| Phase | Motion | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Design partners (hand-recruited) | 3-10 reference customers; prove the wedge works in production |
| 2 | Content / SEO + marketplace listings | Repeatable low-CAC inbound; rank on India-tax-and-Tally queries |
| 3 | Channel partners (Tally resellers, CAs) | Referral pipeline at ₹50k-1.5L CAC |
| 4 | Light inside sales | Convert inbound and partner leads; only after phases 2-3 produce volume |

<p class="source-note">Source: our own derivation, anchored to THESIS GTM stance and upGrowth channel CAC ranges (medium confidence).</p>

## Tie to the ICP

Every channel here points at the same buyer: the Finance-led ICP, 50-500 employees, Tally or ERP-first, field-or-travel-heavy, with the CFO or Finance Controller as economic buyer and the Finance/AP or HR-Ops lead as champion. Content topics, partner relationships, and marketplace listings all index on that buyer's pains and habits. Read the full committee and qualifier in [buyers & ICP](/users/buyers-icp/). The channels are cheap precisely because they ride the buyer's existing accounting relationships rather than interrupting strangers.

:::tip[The verdict]
<span class="pill pill--build">Build</span> a content-plus-channel-plus-founder-led motion. <span class="pill pill--avoid">Avoid</span> outbound SDRs and ABM until ACVs and a proven funnel justify them. The affordable channels are not a compromise; for a software-only indie at India SMB price points, they are the only channels whose math works.
:::
