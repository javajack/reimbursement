# VERIFY: "Happay was acquired by CRED (2021); Fyle is a US-India expense startup; Zoho Expense has transparent low pricing; Volopay/EnKash/Kodo bundle corporate cards + expense; Zaggle/Pluxee/Sodexo do meal & benefit cards." — verify current status, ownership, funding, and public pricing (2024-2026) for these India expense-reimbursement players.

- **Verdict:** partially-confirmed  (confidence: high)
- **Corrected / best-supported:** The market-structure framing is broadly correct, but two ownership facts are STALE and one branding fact needs updating:

1. HAPPAY: CRED did acquire Happay in 2021 (~$180M), but CRED then SOLD Happay's expense-management business to MakeMyTrip (MMT). Announced 18 Nov 2024 via a Business Transfer Agreement; deal closed in early 2025. Happay now sits inside MMT's corporate-travel suite alongside myBiz and Quest2Travel. Notably, Happay's PAYMENTS business/team stayed with CRED — only the expense-management brand/team moved to MMT. So "acquired by CRED (2021)" is no longer the current ownership.

2. FYLE: Correct that it was a US-India (Bengaluru-founded, Newark NJ ops) expense startup — but it was ACQUIRED BY SAGE in July 2025 and rebranded "Sage Expense Management." It is no longer an independent startup. Total funding before acquisition was ~$15.4M.

3. ZOHO EXPENSE: Pricing is genuinely transparent and low. Current India INR pricing: Free (up to 3 users), Standard ₹99/user/mo (₹79 annual), Premium ₹199/user/mo (₹149 annual), plus Custom/Enterprise. Min 5 users on paid plans; prices exclude GST. (US pricing is separate: ~$4-8/active user/mo.) CONFIRMED.

4. VOLOPAY/ENKASH/KODO bundle corporate cards + expense — CONFIRMED. Funding: Volopay ~$33.5M total (largest: $29M Series A, Mar 2022; ex-Revolut CEO James Radford appointed CEO Mar 2025). EnKash ~$23M (≈₹190 Cr); launched a unified corporate-card ecosystem Oct 2025. Kodo ~$9.5M total (Y Combinator + Brex-backed $8.75M seed); small (~35 staff, ₹5.4 Cr revenue FY25) but operating, not shut down. CAUTION: a widely-cited "$77.3M ARR" figure for Volopay (Latka/getlatka) is self-reported and implausible for its funding/headcount — treat as unverified.

5. ZAGGLE/PLUXEE/SODEXO do meal & benefit cards — CONFIRMED with updates. ZAGGLE is publicly listed (IPO Sep 2023, listed at ₹164 on NSE: ZAGGLE / BSE: 543985); FY25 revenue ~₹1,303 Cr (+68% YoY), PAT ~₹87.5 Cr; market cap ~₹2,686 Cr (down ~54% over the prior year as of early 2026). "SODEXO" benefits in India is now PLUXEE — Sodexo's Benefits & Rewards Services spun off and listed on Euronext Paris (ticker PLX) on 1 Feb 2024; Indian entity is "Pluxee (formerly Sodexo BRS)." So listing Sodexo and Pluxee as separate players is wrong — they are the same business under a new name/owner.

## Reasoning

I independently verified each entity via primary/credible secondary sources. The claim's category map (cards+expense bundlers vs. meal/benefit card issuers vs. SaaS expense tools) holds up, but three items are stale or imprecise: (a) Happay's ownership moved CRED→MakeMyTrip (2024/25), with the payments arm retained by CRED; (b) Fyle ceased to be an independent startup when Sage acquired it in July 2025; (c) "Sodexo" and "Pluxee" are not two players — Pluxee IS the rebranded/spun-off Sodexo benefits business (Euronext-listed Feb 2024). I confirmed Zaggle's public-listing status and pulled current financials. Zoho's India INR pricing was fetched directly from Zoho's India pricing page and is transparent/low as claimed. I flagged one unreliable revenue figure (Volopay "$77.3M ARR") as self-reported and inconsistent with its modest funding (~$33.5M) and headcount (~140), so it should not be cited as fact. Confidence is high on ownership/listing/funding events (multiple independent confirmations) and on Zoho pricing (vendor page); slightly lower only on exact private-company revenue figures.
