# VERIFY: "The India expense-management software market is large and fast-growing." (SaaS feasibility study on India expense-reimbursement software — verify market size with analyst reports including year & CAGR, separate credible data from vendor marketing, give a defensible range, and sanity-check via bottoms-up.)

- **Verdict:** partially-confirmed  (confidence: medium)
- **Corrected / best-supported:** India expense/T&E management software market ≈ USD 120-350 million today (point estimate ~USD 150-250M), growing ~14-18% CAGR; "fast-growing" confirmed, "large" overstated. MRFR's ~USD 593M-by-2025 India figure is an outlier/ceiling, not consensus.

## Reasoning

DIRECTION OF SKEPTICISM: vendor-adjacent syndicated reports cluster around inflated, internally inconsistent India numbers, so I discounted the high end. EVIDENCE: (1) Global anchor is solid and consistent across two independent firms — Mordor Intelligence puts global expense-management software at USD 8.48B (2026) → 13.82B (2031) at 10.1% CAGR; Fortune Business Insights (FBI) puts it at USD 8.33B (2025) → 17.26B (2034) at ~8.3% CAGR. Both flag APAC as the fastest region (~17.1% CAGR) with India/e-invoicing/GST as drivers. (2) India-specific data is thin and divergent: MRFR claims India ~USD 593.3M by 2025 at 14.1% CAGR — but MRFR's own GLOBAL figure for the same year is only USD 6,599.2M, which would make India ~9% of the entire global market. That is implausibly high given India is ~2-4% of global enterprise-software spend (Grand View: India enterprise software USD 11.5B in 2024 vs a much larger global base), so I treat MRFR India as overstated. FBI's India breakout is far smaller — India "gains USD 0.26B in 2026" for broad expense management and "USD 0.19B by 2026" for travel-and-expense (T&E) specifically — i.e. roughly USD 190-260M, which is more defensible and consistent with India being a single-digit-percent slice of a USD 8B+ global market. (3) CATEGORY CAVEAT: "expense management software" vs "travel & expense (T&E)" vs "spend management" are different scopes (Grand View global T&E: USD 3.75B in 2023 → 10.69B by 2030 at 16.9% CAGR; global spend management ~USD 3.65B in 2024 → 5.54B by 2030 at 7.2% per Valuates/TBRC) — vendors blur these, inflating apparent size. BOTTOMS-UP SANITY CHECK: India has ~2.8M registered companies, ~1.8M active (MCA, Jan 2025), ~27% in business services. Realistic near/mid-term SaaS-addressable formal segment ≈ 50k-150k companies (those with travel/reimbursement workflows willing to pay for SaaS). Indian expense SaaS lists at ₹250-500 per-employee-per-month (Zoho Expense ₹250-500, Fyle ₹500+); a ~30-50 reimbursing-employee company at ~₹350 PEPM ≈ ₹1-2 lakh/yr ≈ USD 1.3-2.5k ACV (enterprise deals far higher). 100k companies × ~USD 1.5k ≈ USD 150M; bull case 150k × USD 2k ≈ USD 300M. This brackets the credible top-down (USD 120-350M) and sits well under the MRFR USD 593M headline, confirming the headline is high. NET: claim's growth half is true; the "large" framing should be qualified to "small but fast-growing niche (low hundreds of USD millions)." Confidence is medium because India-only analyst figures are sparse, syndicated, and not independently audited.
