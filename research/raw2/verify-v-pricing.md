## Reasoning

Verified each exemplar against primary or near-primary sources. Intercom: fin.ai/pricing confirms $0.99 per outcome (outcome = end-to-end resolution or procedure ending in handoff), 50-outcome monthly minimum on non-Intercom helpdesks, seats from $29 — the dossier's 'per resolution' is the pre-2025 term. Zapier: zapier.com/pricing and help.zapier.com confirm task-based metering persists (no move to credits), with pay-per-task overage at 1.25x and free internal steps. DocuSign: ecom.docusign.com confirms envelope allowances (5/mo Personal; 100/user/yr Standard & Business Pro) atop per-seat fees with pay-as-you-go envelope overage — so 'envelope-based plans' is directionally right but it's a seat+allowance hybrid. Slack: official help pages confirm 90-day visible history, older messages hidden and restored on upgrade (value-withheld), Pro $7.25/user/mo annual; however the Aug 26, 2024 policy deleting free-workspace data >1 year old means withheld value now decays — a material staleness in the claim; 'end-user pressure on admins' is standard PLG interpretation, plausible but not a falsifiable stat. Surveys: OpenView's State of UBP 2nd ed. (Feb 2023) is the source of the 61%-adoption majority figure; OpenView data shows UBP companies with ~120% NDR vs ~110% subscription-only (top quartile 122% vs 109%), and Chargebee's 2025 State of Recurring Revenue report shows 43% hybrid adoption (61% projected end-2026) and 38% higher NRR for hybrid — so 'majority + higher NRR' is supported, but only OpenView supplies the majority number and it's a 2023 figure from a firm that shut down Dec 2023. WhatsApp: Meta's developer docs (fetched via render proxy) confirm per-message pricing effective July 1, 2025 and current rate cards effective April 1, 2026; Twilio confirms utility templates are free inside the 24h service window. India utility ₹0.115 is confirmed for the July 2025 rate card (Authkey/a2zsms additionally report utility/auth unchanged at Jan 1, 2026 while marketing rose ₹0.7846→₹0.8631); AiSensy and Chati both quote ₹0.145 utility / ₹1.09 marketing for 2026 (a uniform ~26% uplift suggesting either the April 2026 card or GST/markup-inclusive quoting). Meta's authoritative CSV rate card is hash-protected and could not be downloaded, so the precise current base utility rate is bounded at ₹0.115–0.145; everything else is pinned to primary sources.
