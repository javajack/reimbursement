# Unsolved pain points in pure-software employee expense reimbursement (capture -> OCR -> approve -> reimburse), mined from G2/Capterra/TrustRadius/app stores/forums for Zoho Expense, Expensify, SAP Concur, Fyle, Happay, Rydoo, ExpenseOnDemand — with India-specific gaps

## Executive Summary

Across review sites, app stores, vendor KBs, and bug trackers, the same software-only failures recur in 2023-2026 and none of the seven incumbents has fully solved them. The most damaging cluster is capture+OCR: receipts photographed blurry/wrinkled fail silently or — worse — produce confidently wrong data (an open 2024 Expensify bug shows blurry receipts yielding a 'random amount' with no warning flag), SmartScan-style async OCR officially takes 'a few minutes to a few hours', and Concur shipped a months-long iPhone 15 blurry-camera regression that users worked around by switching phones; no major incumbent ships document-scanner-grade edge-detection capture in its core flow (Emburse/Payhawk market it as a differentiator). Second is the approval black hole: manager inboxes are the admitted #1 bottleneck (Fyle's own blog), rejections often arrive without reasons (Happay G2), rejected reports vanish from AP visibility (Concur forum), and whole-report resubmission punishes employees for single-line errors. Third is reimbursement opacity and speed — a 2024 Webexpenses survey found 43% of UK employees say slow reimbursement hurts their work experience, Indian vendor data claims 8+ day manual cycles, and Happay Play Store reviewers report money 'withdrawn but not disbursed' — confirming 'when do I get paid?' is unanswered even inside the apps. Fourth, pricing models are actively resented: per-user fees plus scan metering (Expensify's 25 free SmartScans/month, $0.20 overage) and Trustpilot-documented overbilling/cancellation traps validate a submissions-metered, unlimited-user model with a soft paywall. India-specific software gaps are concrete: ITC-grade GST capture (employer GSTIN on invoice, CGST/SGST/IGST split) is rarely automated — one vendor pegs up to 60% of ITC on travel spend lost — and Zoho's autoscan language list does not include Hindi or any Indic language. Finance-admin pains (no search-by-amount in Expensify, missing MIS in Happay, no clean Tally JV export, GL-mapping failures in Zoho) and mobile reliability (Happay OTP login failures, Concur upload 'server errors', lost offline drafts) round out the attack surface. GBTA's widely cited baseline ($58 to process a report, 19% error rate, $52+18 minutes per fix) quantifies the cost of every one of these failure loops. Net: a software-only product wins by making capture deterministic (edge-detected, de-skewed, instant-feedback), OCR honest (confidence flags + graceful manual line-item fallback), approvals escalation-driven with per-line rejection, payout status explicit, and pricing per-submission — each of which maps to a documented, unresolved complaint.

## Findings

- **Blurry/poor receipt photos cause OCR to emit confidently WRONG data rather than failing safely — Expensify's own bug tracker shows blurry receipts producing a 'random amount' with no warning indicator** _(high)_
  GitHub issue #45904 'mWeb - Scan - Creating scan expense using blurry receipt shows random amount' (reported Jul 22, 2024, v9.0.10, reproducible in production on Android/iOS/mWeb/desktop). Expected: red dot indicator + amount 0.00; Actual: 'a random/incorrect amount displays after scanning completes'. Separate Expensify community thread 'Smartscan wrong amount' reports employees reimbursed wrong amounts because no flag prompts double-checking.

- **Camera capture quality is an unsolved, device-dependent failure: SAP Concur shipped a months-long iPhone 15 blurry-receipt regression; users' workaround was switching phones** _(high)_
  Apple Support Communities thread (Oct 31, 2023): on iPhone 15 Pro 'all images are blurry and concur is unable to read the expense receipts'; May 2024 follow-up user 'ultimately switching to a Samsung work phone' because 'the only way it works is when I pull the phone away quite a distance'. ## UX Patterns

- Async OCR queue with 'Scanning...' placeholder (Expensify SmartScan): fails because waits run minutes-to-hours and failures surface as unflagged wrong values; Expensify's own spec in GitHub #45904 (red-dot indicator + 0.00 amount on low confidence) is the right pattern but unimplemented — ship per-field confidence flags and a guaranteed-time or instant fallback instead
- Scan-failed triage bucket (Zoho 'Scan Failed Receipts' list, tap to add manually or delete): works as a graceful soft-fail and directly validates the planned manual-line-item fallback at submission
- Capture coaching via help text ('flat, dark surface, hold steady' — Expensify): anti-pattern that exports the problem to the user; the proven fix is live edge detection + auto-capture + de-skew (Evernote Scannable auto-snaps; Emburse 'smart border cropping' with manual border adjust; Payhawk AI camera) — none of the big-7 core apps do this, which is the capture wedge
- Multiple receipts in one photo silently breaks OCR (Zoho KB): camera flow should detect and split or warn at capture time, not fail downstream
- Email-in receipts with silent drop on unverified sender (Expensify): always bounce with a reason; Expensify also created broken expenses from its own failure notifications (GitHub #71911) — dedupe by message-ID and never ingest own system mail
- Whole-report bounce on rejection (Concur/most): per-line rejection with reason codes (Declaree pattern) keeps the rest of the report moving and kills the $52/18-minute correction loop
- Approval reminders as admin-configured emails (Concur): default-on persistent reminders + auto-escalation to next approver after N days (ExpensePoint/Oracle pattern) beats reminder spam; Fyle reviews show undifferentiated notification volume trains users to ignore the tool
- Rejected reports vanishing from AP view until resubmission (Concur): keep a shared, immutable status timeline visible to employee, approver, and finance simultaneously
- No payout ETA anywhere (category-wide): show a status timeline 'submitted -> approved -> in payout batch -> paid' with expected pay date (Spendesk markets exactly this); with bank bulk-file export, surface 'included in file generated on <date>' as the paid-proxy event
- Forced hotel itemization with rigid math (Concur): if line totals must equal header total, auto-distribute residuals and allow per-night rate variance in one expense — or extract line items from the folio via OCR so the employee only confirms
- Scan-credit hard paywall mid-month (Expensify 25 free scans 'exhausted within 2 weeks', $0.20 overage; Zoho autoscan silently removed from free plan): validates the planned soft paywall — accept and queue uploads, pause intelligent processing, let employees nudge admins, never block submission
- Duplicate detection keyed to a single channel (Fyle card-lag duplicates): fingerprint receipts across camera/email/manual (merchant+date+amount+image hash) with a 'merge' suggestion instead of hard block or silent double-entry

- The India-specific quantitative claims (60% ITC loss, 8+ day cycles, 32% faster) come from vendor marketing blogs, not audited studies — use as directional, not citable-as-fact
- GBTA's $58/19%/$52 figures originate in a 2015 GBTA/HRS study; they remain the industry-cited baseline in 2024-2026 sources but are dated
- JustUseApp 'safety scores' (Expensify 35.2/100, Concur 0/100) are NLP-derived composites, not editorial ratings — cite only as sentiment signals

- Several cited Expensify community URLs are from a forum frozen July 31, 2024; pages may disappear (one already returns 403) — archive copies before publication
- Complaint mining over-samples angry users; ratings for these products remain broadly positive (e.g., Expensify ~4.2-4.7 on app stores per secondary sources), so pains are wedges, not proof incumbents are failing companies wholesale

## Recommendations

- Make capture deterministic, not coached: live edge detection, auto-capture, de-skew, glare/blur detection with immediate retake prompt in the PWA camera flow — every incumbent failure here (Concur iPhone 15 blur, Zoho wrinkled-receipt fails) is a cheap demo-able win
- Engineer OCR for honesty over magic: per-field confidence with visible flags, hard rule that low-confidence never auto-fills amounts (the exact bug Expensify shipped), and one-tap fallback to manual line items at submission — position 'never silently wrong' as the trust claim
- Ship approvals with escalation and per-line rejection ON by default: N-day auto-escalate up the chain, mandatory rejection reason, reject single lines not whole reports, and a shared status view so finance can always see rejected items
- Answer 'when do I get paid?' structurally: employee-visible timeline with expected payout date driven by the bank-file export event; this is the cheapest retention feature in the category and incumbents demonstrably lack it
- Build GST-grade capture as the India moat: GSTIN field extraction + format validation, CGST/SGST/IGST split, flag invoices missing employer GSTIN as 'ITC at risk' for finance, and train/handle Hindi+major Indic-language receipts that Zoho's OCR explicitly cannot read
- Lead pricing with the anti-Expensify story: unlimited users/approvers free, metered submissions, soft paywall that queues instead of blocks, no annual lock-in tricks — Trustpilot billing complaints are ready-made sales ammunition
- Treat Tally JV export + search-by-amount + MIS (processed/failed/pending counts) as launch-blocking finance features, not roadmap items — they are the buyer-persona complaints at Expensify, Happay, and Zoho
- Make email-in receipts loud-failing: verified senders with explicit bounce-back reasons, cross-channel dedupe, and a visible 'received but unprocessed' queue so nothing is silently dropped
