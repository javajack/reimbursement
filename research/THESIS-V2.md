# THESIS v2 — the spine of the Kharcha v2 dossier

Every page must agree with this file. It supersedes THESIS.md (v1) where they differ; v1 remains valid for market/regulatory figures explicitly carried below. Where a number is soft, carry its provenance and confidence.

## What changed from v1 (the reframe)

v1 asked "is this worth building and how." v2 fixes the answer into a concrete two-phase, pure-software product and goes build-deep on Phase 1. The verdict stands: <strong>conditional build</strong>, software-only, India-first, monetised on software (now: metered submissions, not seats), money movement never on our books.

## Product definition (canonical)

**Phase 1 (the product): employee reimbursement, end to end.**
- **Capture (the PWA):** employees scan bills with a mobile-web/PWA camera flow engineered to document-scanner grade: true-aspect-ratio viewfinder (no stretched bills), automatic edge detection, perspective correction and de-skew, glare/blur guidance, multi-page bills, client-side compression, offline-tolerant upload queue. Photo-library upload and email-in are secondary intake paths.
- **OCR to itemised structure:** every bill becomes structured line items (merchant, date, GSTIN if present, line items with amounts, tax split, total) with field-level confidence; the employee reviews and fixes before submitting.
- **Manual fallback:** if extraction fails or the bill is hopeless (faded thermal, handwritten), the employee enters line items manually, one at a time, with smart defaults. Same downstream flow; the image stays attached as evidence.
- **The Console (web app):** employees can also submit on web; approvers get an inbox with one-tap decisions inside the PWA/web Console, reached by web push (installed PWA) or email notification, plus signed magic-link Approve / Send-back buttons in transactional email; finance gets a verification queue, payout-batch builder, exports, and reports; admins get onboarding and configuration. The whole product is a PWA for every role now; native mobile apps consuming the same API are the future path. We do NOT use WhatsApp or any third-party messaging channel for anything.

**Notification channels (canon):** exactly three in Phase 1: `in_app` (a notifications inbox in the PWA/Console for every role), `web_push` (installed PWA; Android Chrome fully, iOS only when added to Home Screen on 16.4+, so best-effort there), and `email` (universal, transactional, the guaranteed channel). A fourth, `native_push` (FCM/APNs), is reserved for the future native apps over the API. No `whatsapp` channel exists anywhere.
- **Approvals:** maker-checker, multi-level, routed by configurable amount thresholds and org relationships (manager chains, department approvers, finance final), with SLA timers, reminders, escalation, delegation/out-of-office, send-back-with-query, and line-item partial approval.
- **Company onboarding & configuration:** a guided wizard plus a configuration model that makes the same software fit a 20-person agency and a 500-person field-sales company: org structure, categories, policies, approval matrix, GST options, payout setup, notification channels. Templates by company type; sane defaults; simulate-before-activate.
- **Credits & metering (pricing as product):** unlimited users and approvers, free, forever. The meter is **submissions per company**. When credits exhaust: capture and upload continue (nothing is ever lost), intelligent processing and workflow entry pause in a visible queue, employees get a one-tap "remind your admin" nudge, admins get burn-rate forecasts and top-up paths. Top-up instantly drains the queue FIFO.
- **Payouts (pure-software invariant):** default is a **bank bulk-payment file** generated per the customer's bank format, executed by the customer in their own net-banking; optional per-customer **payout-API integration (Razorpay-style)** for one-click disbursement; optional **reimburse-via-payroll** export. Funds NEVER touch our accounts.

**Phase 2 (separate, later): vendor reimbursement and payouts.** Vendor KYC (PAN/GSTIN/bank/Udyam), vendor-invoice claims with TDS/RCM handling, vendor payout batches, the MSME 45-day clock, AP-automation adjacency. Documented as overview + deltas, not mixed into Phase 1.

**Non-goals (both phases):** no corporate cards, no prepaid instruments, no float or lending, no holding funds, no travel booking, no native apps in Phase 1 (PWA first; optional TWA/Capacitor wrapper later if Play-Store presence is demanded).

## Canonical product contracts (use these exact names everywhere)

### Entities (PascalCase; table names snake_case plural)
Company, User, Membership (User↔Company with roles), Department, CostCenter, Grade, Claim, LineItem, Receipt, Advance, ApprovalChain, ApprovalStep, Policy, Rule, CreditEntry (the credit ledger), PayoutBatch, PayoutInstruction, BankAccount (company source accounts), BeneficiaryAccount (employee payout account, verification-gated), AuditEvent, Notification, ApiKey, WebhookEndpoint.

Roles (Membership.roles, array): `owner`, `admin`, `finance`, `approver`, `employee`, `auditor` (read-only). A user can hold several.

### Claim lifecycle (Claim.status)
`draft` → `awaiting_credits` (soft paywall; only when company balance is 0 at submit) → `submitted` (credit consumed) → `in_review` → (`sent_back` → resubmit to `submitted`, free) → `approved` | `rejected` → `in_payout` → `paid` → `closed`; `cancelled` from any pre-decision state. Line items individually end `approved | capped | rejected`; a claim with any approved line proceeds with `approved_amount_paise ≤ claimed_amount_paise`. Clawback/recovery is a separate process record, not a claim state.

### ApprovalStep.status
`pending` → `active` → `approved` | `rejected` | `sent_back` | `skipped` | `escalated` | `delegated` | `expired`.

### Receipt.status
`uploaded` → `queued` (credits exhausted or backlog) → `processing` → `extracted` | `extraction_failed` (→ manual entry path; image stays attached).

### PayoutBatch.status
File path: `building` → `exported` → `executed` (customer confirms / return file) → `reconciled`. API path: `building` → `executing` → `completed` | `partially_failed` → `reconciled`. `cancelled` while building.

### Credits (the meter)
- **1 credit = 1 claim submitted into the approval workflow.** OCR at capture (up to 5 receipt images per claim), storage, approvals, payout-file generation, and exports are all inside that credit. Beyond 5 images in one claim: +1 credit per additional 5 (the anti-batching fair-use guard, stated plainly in the pricing FAQ; DocuSign envelope-stuffing precedent).
- MANUAL-entry submissions consume a credit too (the meter is the submission and its workflow, not the OCR); failed OCR never consumes an extra credit.
- Resubmission after `sent_back` is FREE (same claim, same credit). Cancelled claims are not auto-refunded; admins can adjust via ledger `adjustment`.
- Pre-submission OCR on unsubmitted receipts is free but fair-use capped (max(20, 3 × remaining credits) scans/month) to bound free-tier COGS.
- CreditEntry.type: `plan_grant` (monthly allowance, expires at period end, no rollover), `pack_purchase` (prepaid packs, 12-month validity), `consumption`, `expiry`, `adjustment`, `promo_grant`. Balance = sum(ledger). Consumption order: expiring-soonest first.
- Soft paywall at balance 0: uploads accepted (`queued`), submissions land in `awaiting_credits`, processing paused, employee nudge ("remind your admin", one tap), admin alerts at 80% and 100% burn plus a burn-rate forecast ("out in ~N days"). Top-up auto-drains the queue FIFO. Optional auto-top-up with a monthly spend cap.

### Plans (canonical names; final prices live in /strategy/pricing/)
`Free`, `Starter`, `Growth`, `Scale` (custom), plus prepaid **Credit Packs** and optional **auto-top-up**. Unlimited seats on every plan including Free. Plan = monthly included submissions at a declining effective per-credit price; packs cover spikes. GST 18% extra on all prices.

### Capture pipeline stage names
`intake` (capture ladder below) → `frame_analysis` (enhanced viewfinder only: edge detection, stability, glare/blur checks) → `capture` (auto when stable or manual shutter; or native-camera return on the file-input path) → `quality_gate` (blur via variance-of-Laplacian + brightness histogram; retake prompt) → `crop_correct` (perspective correction + de-skew; server-side in v1 with a client manual 4-corner rescue editor) → `review` (retake / add page / multi-page tray) → `compress` (client-side, ~2048 px long edge JPEG q≈0.8, ~200-500 KB/page, EXIF-safe canvas re-encode) → `queue_upload` (IndexedDB outbox, offline-tolerant) → `upload` → server `extract` (OCR) → `review_items` (field/line-item review UX) → attach to Claim.

**The capture ladder (canonical):** (1) `<input type="file" accept="image/jpeg,image/png" capture="environment">` is the UNIVERSAL PRIMARY path: full-resolution native camera app, zero permission prompts, works in every in-app browser and on iOS standalone (where WebKit bug 252465 still intermittently kills getUserMedia streams). (2) The ENHANCED VIEWFINDER (getUserMedia + live edge overlay + auto-capture) is progressive enhancement, Android-first, feature-detected at runtime, never required. (3) `ImageCapture.takePhoto()` where present (Chrome 59+; Safari/iOS 18.4+, Mar 2025) for full-sensor stills; else canvas grab at stream resolution.

**The no-distortion rule:** stream frames are undistorted at the stream's intrinsic `videoWidth`/`videoHeight`; the stretched-bill bug is ALWAYS a presentation/capture bug. Canonical pattern: request `ideal` constraints → wait for `loadedmetadata` and read `videoWidth`/`videoHeight` → preview with an explicit box + `object-fit: cover` (crops, never stretches; never `fill`) → capture onto a canvas sized exactly `videoWidth × videoHeight` (never from CSS pixels or devicePixelRatio) → for WYSIWYG vs the cover-cropped preview use 9-arg `drawImage` with a source-rect crop matching the box aspect. Dev builds assert capture aspect == stream aspect. Spec lives in /phase-1/capture/camera/ and /spec/capture-pipeline/.

### API conventions
REST JSON under `/api/v1`. Resources kebab-case in paths (`/claims/{claim_id}/line-items`), fields snake_case. Money is integer **paise** (`amount_paise`) with `currency` (INR default). Timestamps ISO 8601 UTC. IDs are prefixed ULIDs: `co_`, `usr_`, `mem_`, `clm_`, `li_`, `rcp_`, `apr_` (step), `adv_`, `pob_` (batch), `pin_` (instruction), `cre_` (credit entry), `ba_`, `bene_`, `rule_`, `evt_`. Auth: session JWT for apps, scoped API keys for integrations, `Idempotency-Key` honored on all POSTs. Cursor pagination (`cursor`, `limit ≤ 100`). Errors are RFC 7807 `application/problem+json`. Webhooks HMAC-SHA256 signed (`X-Kharcha-Signature`).

### Rule engine (carried from v1, scoped to Phase 1)
Rules are data: `{ id, name, scope (line_item | claim | advance), enabled, priority, condition (AND/OR tree of {field, operator, value}), action (allow | flag | warn | cap | block | route), severity, hardness (hard|soft), params, message, effective_from/to, version }`. Exactly six action verbs. Deterministic order: line_item rules per line, then claim rules; `block` overrides softer outcomes; `cap` sets line `approved_amount_paise = min(claimed, cap)`; partial coverage of a mixed bill falls out of line-item evaluation. India-native built-ins: GSTIN_FORMAT (Luhn-mod-36), PER_DIEM_CAP (city-tier × grade), RECEIPT_REQUIRED_ABOVE, DUPLICATE_SUSPECT, ITC_ELIGIBLE (tags eligible vs blocked under Sec 17(5); informational for finance in Phase 1). Every firing is an AuditEvent with rule id+version and input snapshot. Vendor scopes (TDS/RCM) arrive in Phase 2.

### Stack (canonical recommendation; argued in /spec/architecture/)
TypeScript end-to-end. Backend: Node (NestJS or Hono) modular monolith + worker process; PostgreSQL 16 with row-level security per tenant; Redis (or pg-boss) job queue; S3-compatible object storage for receipt images (ap-south-1 / Mumbai region); LLM-vision OCR behind a provider-agnostic interface (Gemini Flash family or Claude Haiku class) wrapped in deterministic validation (GSTIN checksum, arithmetic totals, date sanity). Frontend: one React + Vite codebase, responsive, a PWA serving EVERY role (employee capture, approver inbox, finance console, admin) as routes of the same app. Notifications: transactional email (provider-agnostic, SES/Postmark/Resend-class) plus web push (VAPID/Web Push Protocol); native-app push (FCM/APNs) over the API arrives with the future native apps. No WhatsApp or third-party messaging dependency. Single region, containers, boring and observable.

## v2 research-derived canon (10 fresh studies + 3 adversarial checks, Jun 2026)

### The unresolved software-only pains (the demand evidence; full detail in raw2/research-pain-points-software.md)
1. **Capture fails silently or wrongly.** Expensify's open bug #45904: blurry receipts produce a confidently WRONG "random amount" with no warning flag. Concur shipped a months-long iPhone 15 blurry-camera regression (users switched phones). No big-7 incumbent ships document-scanner-grade edge-detection capture in its core mobile flow; they coach users ("flat, dark surface, hold steady") instead. [high]
2. **OCR is slow and dishonest.** SmartScan officially takes "a few minutes to a few hours"; misreads force re-keying; forced hotel itemisation is the most hated data-entry task (Concur forums). [high/medium]
3. **The approval black hole.** Manager inbox is the admitted #1 bottleneck; rejections arrive without reasons; rejected reports vanish from AP visibility; whole-report bounce punishes one bad line. Concur lacks manager-level bulk approval. [high]
4. **"When do I get paid?" is unanswered in-product**, category-wide. 43% of employees say slow reimbursement hurts their work experience (Webexpenses UK 2024); ~9-day average claim processing. [medium]
5. **Pricing is resented**: per-user fees + scan meters (Expensify 25 free SmartScans/mo then $0.20; Zoho 20 autoscans) + billing traps. Validates unlimited-seats + metered-submissions + soft paywall. [high]
6. **India gaps**: GST/ITC-grade capture (employer GSTIN, CGST/SGST/IGST split) rarely automated (vendor claim: up to 60% of T&E ITC lost, directional); Zoho's 14-15 autoscan languages include ZERO Indic scripts (Cloud Vision has 9 GA Indic languages); no clean Tally JV export; finance lacks search-by-amount/MIS. [medium-high]
- Cost baseline to quote (labelled 2015-vintage GBTA, still industry-cited): ~$58 and 20 min to process a report; 19% contain errors; +$52 and +18 min per fix.
- Caveats: review sites were bot-blocked (quotes are search-snippet-mediated; paraphrase risk); India stats (60% ITC, 8+ days) are vendor marketing; incumbents are actively fixing bugs; complaint mining over-samples angry users.

### Capture-tech facts (verified; full detail in raw2/research-mobile-capture-tech.md and verify-v-camera.md)
- Constraints are requests, not guarantees (only exact/min/max bind). Stream frames are NEVER distorted; stretching is always presentation/capture code. The canonical no-distortion pattern is in the contracts above.
- Support matrix (mid-2026): ImageCapture.takePhoto Chrome 59+ / Safari-iOS 18.4+ (Mar 31 2025) / Firefox flag-gated. focusMode/focusDistance Chromium-only (still no iOS). Torch works on iOS since ~17.5.1 (getSettings staleness fixed 18.4); zoom Safari 17.0; whiteBalance 17.4. getUserMedia in installed iOS PWAs since 13.4 BUT WebKit bug 252465 (black/frozen stream in standalone) reproduces through iOS 18.4.1: default to file-input in iOS standalone.
- Edge detection / perspective correction: OpenCV.js default ~7.6-8.4 MB WASM (trimmed 2-4 MB, extreme 0.8 MB); threaded build OOM-crashes 3 GB-RAM phones; jscanify MIT v1.4.2 (active, ~10k weekly downloads) rides OpenCV.js; Dynamsoft from USD 1,249/yr; Scanbot ~EUR 20k/yr (five figures, quote-only). MediaPipe has NO web document scanner (ML Kit is Android-only). v1 decision: perspective correction server-side; client manual 4-corner rescue editor; lazy-load slim OpenCV.js later only on `deviceMemory ≥ 4` devices.
- iOS canvas caps: 16,777,216 px area pre-iOS 18 (67.1M from iOS 18); process ≤12 MP, one page at a time; ~300 MB practical WASM ceiling on mobile.
- Offline queue: IndexedDB outbox records `{blob, submission_id, page_index, idempotency_key, attempts, last_error}`; Workbox Background Sync on Chromium (zero Safari support through 26.x); iOS replays on `online`/`visibilitychange`/app-open; `navigator.storage.persist()`; Safari 7-day script-storage eviction (installed apps exempt). Workbox queues only on network exceptions: the soft-paywall queue is an APPLICATION-layer state (server accepts and marks unprocessed), never the offline-retry queue.
- Preprocessing gains for OCR are real but corpus-dependent: PreP-OCR measured 63.9-70.3% relative CER reduction (historical docs); binarization alone ~27.6% (specialised corpus); popular "15-30%" blog figures are unsourced; Tesseract already binarizes internally (Otsu; Sauvola added in v5). Google Vision wants ≥1024×768, ≤20 MB originals.
- Performance budget (P75 first load <5s on a Galaxy A24-class device): ~150 KiB HTML/CSS + 300-350 KiB JS gzipped. India device reality: Android 93.2% / iOS 6.7% (StatCounter, May 2026); 4-8 GB RAM segment 42.3%.

### OCR-itemisation UX canon (raw2/research-ocr-itemization-ux.md)
- Two latency regimes: on-device coaching must feel instant (<100ms-1s); cloud extraction is ASYNC with an explicit `Scanning` chip on the draft, navigate-away allowed, notify on completion. Target P50 < 5s (Klippa 0.5-4s, Fyle "seconds", vendor-claimed); >10s switches to determinate progress + "we'll notify you" (Nielsen limits). NEVER block the form on OCR.
- Confidence-threshold routing: auto-accept fields ≥ 0.80 (Azure convention); flag only doubtful fields; require explicit confirmation only of merchant + date + total (Expensify's mandatory trio).
- Tap-to-fix with bounding boxes (Rossum pattern): tapping a field zooms the image to its source region; machine values neutral, green only after human touch; Tab/jump to next low-confidence field. Line items edit in a focused bottom row-sheet on mobile (Zoho Itemize pattern), never a dense grid.
- Failure is a recoverable queue (Zoho "Scan Failed Receipts" + its "plan limit exceeded" filter = the literal soft-paywall precedent): per-receipt reasons, bulk retry, one-tap "enter manually" pre-filled with whatever was extracted; merchant-memory smart defaults per tenant.
- Duplicates in three layers: capture-time perceptual-hash, submission-time dismissible warning on date+amount collision with Keep-both/Keep-one verbs (Expensify), silent auto-merge only with tight tolerances (Fyle: amount ε ≤ 0.0002, date window 5 days). Publish the rule (Brex 2-of-3 contract).
- Email-in at launch (per-company unique address, editable prefix; loud bounce-backs, never silent drops); the Android Web Share Target (share an image from the gallery or any app into a claim) is the secondary intake. No messaging-app intake. GST line-item schema: per-line taxable value, rate, CGST/SGST equal-split intrastate or IGST, inclusive/exclusive toggle, Section 170 normal rounding per tax component per invoice; validate CGST == SGST and flag, never silently "fix".

### Onboarding/config canon (raw2/research-onboarding-config.md)
- **5-step wizard, first claim in one session (<15 min):** (1) org basics (India defaults: INR, Apr-Mar FY; GSTIN present but skippable) → (2) company-size approval template (`<50`: manager-only; `50-250`: manager → finance above ₹25,000; `250+`: manager → dept head over threshold → finance) → (3) preloaded India category set with on/off toggles (Local Conveyance, Travel Air/Rail, Lodging, Meals, Fuel/Mileage, Telecom, Office Supplies, Software, Medical, Misc) → (4) invite up to 3 people inline or skip → (5) a pre-seeded DEMO CLAIM the admin approves in-wizard. Bank account, GST detail, branches, bulk import live on a post-wizard checklist; bank details are required before the first PAYOUT RUN, never the first claim.
- Hard-require only three things before the first real claim: one admin, the undeletable always-matching fallback approval rule (Fyle invariant; Zoho's no-match hard-block is the anti-pattern), one active category.
- Approval matrix: role-abstracted slots (Manager, Manager's manager, Department head, Finance, named group; Ramp pattern) + optional one-screen DoA limit grid (approver × max amount, Concur pattern) + priority numbers for multi-match + **Simulate-a-claim** tester (pick employee/amount/category → rendered chain + which rule fired; Oracle pattern) + chain preview on the employee submission screen (Fyle).
- Safety invariants: graph validation (cycles, orphans, deactivated references) on every save; prevent-self-approval ON by default with skip-up (SAP KB 1944022 deadlock is the cautionary tale); claims NEVER blocked at submission for routing reasons (worst case routes to admin queue flagged `rule_gap`); snapshot-at-submission for in-flight claims (Ramp sentence verbatim-able) with explicit admin "re-route in-flight" action (ApprovalMax restart) and policy version history.
- Import: CSV template (name, work email, employee ID, department, manager email, branch, role) with map→validate→fix-in-grid and dry-run preview; dedupe email+employee ID; 7-day magic links (Fyle's 24h too aggressive), Pending state, bulk resend; domain capture (DNS TXT) for self-serve auto-join.
- Benchmarks to design against: median SaaS time-to-value ~1.5 days (we target demo claim in-session, first real claim <24h); median activation 37%; checklist completion ~19% (nothing on the checklist may gate the first claim). Concur's consultant-dependent config reputation is the positioning foil: "first claim in 15 minutes, no consultant."

### Approver-UX canon (raw2/research-approval-ux.md)

Answer with risk-tiering, not pretence: optional auto-approve for in-policy claims under a threshold + default 5% random audit routing (Expensify precedent), exceptions-first queue with approve-recommended / needs-review badges, guided review stepping through flags only, bulk approve restricted to zero-flag items. Default remains human-approves; automation tier is per-company opt-in.

- One-tap channel ladder: (1) the PWA/web approver inbox, reached by web push (installed PWA) or by an email notification, one tap to Approve / Send-back; (2) signed single-use magic-link Approve / Send-back buttons in transactional email (universal; amount-bounded; step-up to the web app above a threshold); (3) Outlook Actionable Messages only when an M365 customer demands (legacy token EOL Jun 8 2026; gateways break cards); (skip Gmail AMP at launch, 100+ emails/day whitelisting bar). No WhatsApp tier. Future native apps add reliable FCM/APNs push.
- Cadence: 48h SLA (24h above the high-value threshold), one mid-SLA nudge, one pre-escalation warning, then auto-escalate primary → backup/delegate → manager/admin; max 2 reminders per item; IST-aware quiet hours; daily digest batching. Delegation: time-bound out-of-office approver (no account access) + standing delegate (audit-attributed "as delegate of") + admin-assigned delegation + company fallback approver so nothing routes to nobody; unroutable items surface on the admin dashboard.
- Period-close sweep: pending-by-approver dashboard, one-click remind-all, approve-by date tied to the close calendar.
- Audit log to SOX-grade even for unlisted SMBs: append-only; actor, role-at-decision-time, action, before/after, timestamp, IP/device, CHANNEL (`web` / `web_push` / `email_link`, and `native_push` once the apps ship); submitter ≠ approver enforced server-side; 7-8 year retention.

### PWA-in-India stance (raw2/research-pwa-india.md)
- **Staged delivery (canonical): Stage 0** responsive mobile web with file-input capture (works on 100% of devices including third-party in-app browsers; nothing gates on install). **Stage 1** installable PWA serving every role, Android-first GA posture: WebAPK install (deferred prompt AFTER first successful submission), Web Share Target (share an image from the gallery or any app straight into a claim; Android-only), web push for approval and credit nudges, offline outbox, `storage.persist()`. **Stage 2** TWA via Bubblewrap into Play Store when customer IT demands store presence ($25 one-time; mind Play's Jul 2024 minimum-functionality policy). **Stage 3** native apps (Capacitor or native) over the API when reliable cross-platform push or native camera UX justifies it; this is the planned mobile future, and where FCM/APNs push replaces best-effort web push, especially on iOS.
- iOS posture: works in Safari; A2HS coaching only (no install prompt API); never gate on install; notifications fall back to email (and in-app) where web push is unavailable on non-installed iOS; session continuity across the install boundary via magic link/OTP on first standalone launch (Safari and installed app share no cookies); file-input capture default in standalone (bug 252465).
- Vernacular from day one: Hindi + 2-3 state languages, icon-first, numeric-keypad-first forms (98% of India's 886M internet users consume Indic-language content; 55% rural); same-day language fixes are a web-stack sales point against store-release trains.
- Flipkart Lite (70% conversion lift) / Ola (200 KB) numbers are 2016-17 vendor-marketing vintage: directional only. No leading Indian expense vendor is PWA-first today: differentiated, not market-validated.
